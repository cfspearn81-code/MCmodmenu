`spawnnpc-1.0.0-mc26.3.jar` is the standalone Spawn NPC client mod for Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.20.1+, and Java 25. It has no Faster dependency and can also be installed alongside Faster. Put it in `mods`, type `/spawnnpc`, and left-click a dummy while holding a diamond block in your main hand to remove it. The adjacent SHA256 verifies the JAR.

NPCs are local to your game, copy your skin/equipment, and clear on world/player changes. They are not server players and are not saved with worlds.
