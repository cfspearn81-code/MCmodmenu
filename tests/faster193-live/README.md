Faster 1.9.3 Aim Lock target-retention regression on a real Minecraft 26.3/Fabric client at 854×480 connected to a disposable vanilla localhost dedicated server, 127.0.0.1:25568, offline OP BridgeLive. The target entities are deterministic client-only RemotePlayer fixtures, not additional network accounts. Production selection, retention, camera alignment, menus and settings run unchanged. The fixture only supplies hold/focus state and normal menu keyboard/click input, observes outgoing packets/camera output, and prepares an isolated arena through ordinary OP commands. It does not test Hypixel or another public server.

The new behavior intentionally replaces the automatic per-tick nearest-target switching validated in the original 1.9.0 test. A valid living target retains its identity while the key is held. Initial acquisition chooses the nearest visible head within 32 blocks and a 90-degree cone. Those limits apply only before acquiring a target. Death permits a new selection, release clears it, and temporary occlusion/invisibility or menu/focus/Free Cam pauses aiming without switching to another player.

results.txt/run-summary.txt record the final live checks:

- Search/Combat switches and letter/function hold bindings, plus existing Bridge and Free Cam bindings.
- No aiming without hold; nearest visible forward acquisition; a closer player cannot steal the lock; crouching head height follows the same target.
- Temporary invisibility and a real block wall pause aiming while retaining the original target; tracking resumes on visibility return. The unobstructed competitor never replaces it.
- A living held target can move behind the original view or beyond the initial acquisition radius without being replaced.
- Target death permits a new nearby target. Release immediately restores manual aiming; another press makes a fresh forward selection instead of preserving the old player.
- A closer player behind the view and players across the map do not qualify for initial acquisition. If only ineligible players exist, the view stays manual until an eligible player enters the forward area.
- P menu, Free Cam and focus loss preserve target identity while pausing rotation. Disabling clears the target even while the key remains held. Temporary entity removal pauses without switching, and a replacement with the same UUID resumes that player. No living eligible targets leaves the view alone.
- Actual render-camera and normalized player view vectors align with the selected interpolated head during tracking frames. Native outgoing look packets match real player yaw/pitch, at most one movement packet occurs per player tick, and zero attack-interaction packets are sent.
